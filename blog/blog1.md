# My First Blog

Salutations. This is my first blog of my life. I'm very new to this, so please don't laugh.

I guess I should start on who am I, since you the viewer came here curious on what is this site and who the author of 
this blog is.

## Who am I?
My name is Felipe Woods, and I was born on April 30, 2001 in Houston, Texas, and I've been a native Houstonian and 
Texan all of my life. I'm currently off right now, but when I'm not off for the winter or summer, I'm a student at the 
University of Houston-Downtown (Go Gators!). This isn't to confuse UHD with UH that most are familiar with (though we 
are part of the same university system). I've been diagnosed with Autism since I was 6 years old, as well as ADHD and 
other things, so I can sometimes come off as quiet and awkward, but when you get to know me on a personal level, I'm 
pretty funny and can get loud at times (sorry-not-sorry).

## My Interests
Well I play video games, pretty much on a regular basis, actually.
I play mostly first-person-shooters, with a specific emphasis on ones that utilize id Software's id Tech engines, from 
id Tech 1 (the Doom engine) to id Tech 4 (the Doom 3 engine). One of my special interests is trains. As a certain meme 
goes: "I like trains." Hmm, nothing happened yet. I guess I'm gonna... [LOUD TRAIN HORN]. I also consider myself to be 
an avid transit user, primarily of METRO, our public transit provider in Houston. I have many complaints of METRO and 
their services and lack of expansion, but I gotta love-em, ykwim. I know the ins and outs of METRO like the back of my 
hand. In fact, I've been a daily visitor to their [website](https://www.ridemetro.org) since the late 2000s when I was 
a little boy. That's how obsessed I was/still am obsessed with METRO. I've used other providers outside of the Houston 
area throughout the years, including Island Transit in Galveston, VIA Metropolitan Transit in San Antonio, CapMetro in 
Austin, Dallas Area Rapid Transit in Dallas, the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) in Chicago, the Metropolitan 
Transportation Authority (the MTA) in New York, and three providers in the San Francisco Bay Area 
(the San Francisco Municipal Railway (Muni) in San Francisco proper, Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) in the Bay Area, and 
AC Transit in the East Bay (Oakland, Richmond, Berkley, etc.)), and Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority 
(WMATA) in the DC metro. 

## Direction of my Blogs
The primary direction that I see myself with these blogs is mostly transit-oriented, with some political tones and 
causes that intersect with my interest in public transit. In a sense, I want to see a world where cars on the streets 
are slim to none, maybe even nationalizing intercity bus transit with Greyhound much like how the US government 
nationalized the country's intercity passenger railroad operations with Amtrak. Or if you want a better thinking of it, 
I am mostly raging against every bad decision METRO has made in the process of their very good plans (something that I 
might get into with future blogs).
