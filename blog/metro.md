# The History of METRO
In 1973, the 63rd Texas State Legislature passed 
[Senate Bill 642](https://www.lrl.texas.gov/legis/BillSearch/billdetails.cfm?legSession=63-0&billTypeDetail=SB&billNumberDetail=642) 
that authorizes the creation of rapid transit authorities in major metropolitan areas in Texas. Thanks to this piece of 
legislature, Houston-area voters authorized the creation of the Metropolitan Transit Authority of Harris County in 1978,
as well as approving a one-cent sales tax collection to fund it, with it fully taking over all bus services previously 
operated by the City of Houston through HouTran in 1979. Maybe someday I'll explain the history of HouTran, except that 
at the time of METRO's founding, they inherited a system in complete disrepair and a history of unreliability. It took 
METRO many years to fix up their bus system and by the 1980s, it improved enough to be much safer, cleaner, and more 
reliable than it previously was under HouTran. In March 2001, METRO broke ground on what would become the METRORail Red 
Line after many years of false starts
